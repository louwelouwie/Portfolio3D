# Chris Louwerse | 3D Portfolio

My software development portfolio is set on an interactive desk modeled in Blender. Open the laptop or phone to explore my projects, skills, background, and contact details.

## Explore the desk

- Drag the scene to look around, then click the laptop or phone to open its apps.
- Pick up the contact card or use the printer to view and download my CV.
- Click the lamp to change the lighting or the headphones to toggle sound. Use the language control to switch between English and Dutch.

Built with SvelteKit, TypeScript, Three.js, Tailwind CSS, and Blender.
